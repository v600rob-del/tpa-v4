Privacy

TPA · Trains, Planes and Automobiles · last updated 18 November 2026

TPA doesn't track you

There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking or advertising cookies. We don't use analytics services and we don't sell or share anything about you.

What your browser remembers

TPA keeps a few small settings on your own device so the map is how you left it: for example whether the Tracking layers menu is open, whether bus stops are switched on, your plane filter, and whether you've already seen a tip. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes them.

What our server sees

Like every website, our server needs your device's internet address to send the map to it. We don't keep a record of who visits. If a connection goes wrong, the server's technical error log may briefly note the address involved; that log is only used to keep TPA running and clears itself automatically.

If you send us a suggestion

The "Got an idea?" box keeps only what you type, what it's about and when it was sent. No name or address is needed, and we don't keep your internet address with it. If you'd like a reply, email us instead.

Other services your browser talks to

  • OpenFreeMap sends the street map pieces straight to your browser.
  • Esri sends the satellite photos straight to your browser, but only if you press Satellite. It is told only that you are on tpalive.co.uk, not which page.
  • Cloudflare helps deliver TPA quickly and protects it from attacks, so it handles the connection between your browser and our server.
  • The Support TPA button takes you to Buy Me a Coffee, whose own privacy policy then applies. Nothing is sent to them unless you click it.
